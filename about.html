about me

✻ Who I Am
I offer a space where your words, silences, and contradictions can be approached with curiosity rather than judgment. A space where you can encounter yourself differently.

My work is rooted in a psychoanalytic orientation that takes seriously the complexity of human experience, the spoken and the unspoken, the remembered and the forgotten, the desires that move us and the patterns that repeat themselves despite our best intentions.

✻ Education & Seminars
I completed a BA in Clinical Psychology, followed by a Master's in Cognitive Science and Psychology, where I focused on how perception, attention, and decision-making shape our lived experience. Alongside this, I pursued psychoanalytic seminars, deepening my understanding of desire, relational patterns, and the symbolic dimensions of life.

✻ Language & Translation
My background in translation and interpretation informs my sensitivity to language: how we speak, how we silence ourselves, and how meaning shifts across contexts. This linguistic awareness is central to my therapeutic work.
